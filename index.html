About me

I'm a researcher and engineer based in Melbourne, Australia, working at the intersection of physiological signal acquisition, embedded real-time systems, and computational neuroscience.

My work spans low-level firmware and RTOS internals — including active contribution to the RTEMS open-source project — through to multi-channel EEG/ECG data pipelines and machine-learning models for environmental prediction.

I care deeply about systems that are correct, reproducible, and understandable. Whether that's a CAN stack running on bare metal or a signal-processing pipeline for neural data, the same rigour applies.
